Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Yapp is a free walkie-talkie app for friends. It is made by Kreatix, an independent personal project. This policy explains what Yapp collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: info@kreatix.dev.

What we collect

  • Email address, to send you sign-in codes and identify your account.
  • Profile: your first name, username and, if you add one, your profile photo. Your friends can see these, and anyone who knows your username can see your name and photo when sending you a friend request.
  • Friends and requests: who you are friends with and pending friend requests.
  • Status: whether you are online and whether Do Not Disturb is on. Your friends can see this.
  • Push token and device platform (iOS or Android), so we can send you notifications.
  • Technical logs: our servers briefly log requests, including IP addresses, to keep the service running and to stop abuse.

The age you enter during sign-up is only checked on your device and is not sent to us. We do not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifiers or analytics, and Yapp has no ads.

Voice

When you hold to talk, your microphone audio is streamed live through our media server to the friend you are talking to. So they don't miss it when their app is closed or offline, each talk is also saved on our server as a short audio clip (up to 60 seconds). A clip is deleted as soon as your friend hears it live or plays it back, and automatically after 7 days if they never do. Deleting your account deletes every clip you sent or received. Audio is encrypted in transit (TLS and DTLS-SRTP) but it is not end-to-end encrypted, which means it passes through our server unencrypted in memory while it is relayed.

The built-in @test account is an automated echo bot. If you talk to it, it holds your clip in memory for up to 30 seconds so it can play it back to you, then discards it.

Waves and notifications

Waves are delivered to your friend in real time and are not stored. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google push services, which receive your push token and the notification text.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We only use these service providers to run Yapp:

  • Our hosting provider in the Netherlands, where Yapp's servers and database run.
  • Expo (650 Industries), for push notifications and app updates.
  • Apple and Google, for delivering notifications and distributing the app.

Sign-in emails are sent from our own mail server. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Server logs are kept only for a short time.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This permanently removes your profile, photo, friendships, friend requests, devices and sessions. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU and UK, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, email info@kreatix.dev. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Yapp is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All connections to Yapp use encryption in transit. Sign-in uses one-time codes and short-lived access tokens. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
